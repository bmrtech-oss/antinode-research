# Glossary

- **ADR:** Architecture decision record: a concise record of a consequential
  design decision and its rationale.
- **Business rule:** A statement constraining or defining a decision,
  operation, or policy in a business domain.
- **Extraction:** The process of identifying candidate rules from source
  material and linking them to evidence.
- **Provenance:** Information recording where an extracted item came from and
  how it was derived or changed.
- **Registered report:** A research format in which questions, methods, and
  analysis plans are specified before results are known.
- **System 1:** In this paper's design, a fast, deterministic rule-evaluation
  path.
- **System 2:** In this paper's design, a slower, reviewable reasoning path for
  ambiguity and exceptions.

Terms are defined for this repository's documents; they do not imply a claim
that all systems use the same implementation.
