## The Process, in Nine Chapters

Part I described the problem. Part II described the materials. Part III describes the method.

This is the heart of the book. The nine chapters that follow walk through the seven stages of the knowledge pipeline—one stage at a time, in detail—and then show how the stages work together in practice.

**Chapter 7 — The Pipeline Overview** introduces the seven stages, explains why they form a network of loops rather than a line, and shows how you can enter the pipeline at any stage.

**Chapter 8 — Selection** examines the allocation of attention: the three criteria (relevance, credibility, novelty), the source evaluation framework, the art of ignoring, and the distinction between directed and exploratory selection.

**Chapter 9 — Pre-processing** examines the work of turning raw information into something usable: cleaning, structuring, contextualizing, and connecting—and why most pipeline failures originate here.

**Chapter 10 — Generation** examines the formation of candidate models and hypotheses: the methods (abduction, analogy, inversion, combination, anomaly), the creative dimension (incubation, play, constraint), and the modal answer problem of AI-assisted generation.

**Chapter 11 — Testing** examines the comparison of models against evidence: prediction, observation, falsification, the Bayesian stance, the discipline of seeking disconfirmation, and the contaminated test set that cannot fail.

**Chapter 12 — Integration** examines the construction of a coherent knowledge base: coherence, consilience, hierarchy, application, the resolution of contradictions, and the three dashboards that each told a different story.

**Chapter 13 — Retain or Discard** examines the discipline of selective memory: the criteria for retention and discard, the mechanisms of forgetting, the forms of pruning, and the retrieval system that hoarded.

**Chapter 14 — Correct** examines the feedback loop that makes the pipeline a learning system: the three levels of correction, error as signal, the psychology of correction, and the two-year belief that was never questioned.

**Chapter 15 — The Pipeline in Practice** offers a practical guide to implementation: the three stages of habit formation, a worked example, five exercises, seven checklists, and the eleven-day failure that taught the author to start small.

By the end of this part, you should have a working method—not just an understanding of the pipeline, but a sense of how to use it, where it fails, and how to build it into a practice.
